# Acme Logistics NV - Company Profile (FICTIONAL, demo use only)

## At a glance
- Belgian logistics group, head office in Ghent. 2,100 employees. Revenue EUR 480M (FY2025).
- Built by acquisition: three business units, each still running its own customer portal.

| Business unit | Since | Services | Customers | Portal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Acme Parcel | founding company | B2C and B2B parcels, returns | 150,000 registered consumers, 4,200 business accounts | Portal A "MyAcme" |
| Brabant Freight | acquired 2021 | pallet and part-load freight (B2B) | 3,100 business accounts | Portal B "FreightView" |
| Flanders Cold Chain | acquired 2023 | temperature-controlled transport for food and pharma | 2,200 business accounts | Portal C "ColdTrack" |

- 1,800 business customers use more than one portal (overlap analysis, Q2 2026).
- Dutch and French are required commercially and legally; English for international shippers.
- Peak season: mid-October to early January, parcel volumes x2.3.

## Strategy 2030 (approved by the executive committee, March 2026)
1. "One Acme" for customers: one brand, one login, one customer record.
2. Reduce group IT run cost by 20% by 2029.
3. Digital self-service as the default channel: 70% of service interactions.
4. Compliance by design: GDPR, NIS2, accessibility.
